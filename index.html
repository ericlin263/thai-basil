Our Philosophy

Roots. Spice.
& Colorado Air.

Thai Basil was born from a simple belief: that the bold, aromatic traditions of Southeast Asia could find a beautiful new home nestled against the Rockies. We source locally, honor authentically, and cook with everything we have.

From slow-braised Thai Curries to wok-kissed pad see ew, every plate is a bridge between two worlds — the ancient flavors of Thailand, Vietnam, and Japan, reimagined for a Colorado table.

Local Sourcing

Farm-fresh ingredients from Colorado's Front Range, supporting local growers year-round.

Authentic Spice

We import our chilis, lemongrass, and galangal directly from Southeast Asia.

Family Recipes

Third-generation recipes passed down through Chef Lin's family in Fuzhou, China.

Community First

Proudly rooted in Colorado Springs since 2005, serving our neighbors every day.
